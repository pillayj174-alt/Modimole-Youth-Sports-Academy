Who we are

A platform for young people to thrive.

Modimolle Youth Sports Academy (MYSA) is a youth-focused sports development initiative built around the belief that every young person deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and reach their potential.

Through structured sporting activities, mentorship and community involvement, MYSA aims to nurture confident, disciplined and responsible young people—on and off the field.

  • Structured youth football development
  • Positive values, teamwork and leadership
  • Pathways to greater sporting opportunities
  • Community participation and social development

Our programmes

Every age. Every opportunity.

Our football development structure is designed to support young players as they learn the game, improve their abilities and progress through age-appropriate training.

FOUNDATION STAGE

Under 13

Building confidence, coordination, teamwork and a love for the game through foundational football skills.

DEVELOPMENT STAGE

Under 15

Developing technical ability, decision-making, discipline and a stronger understanding of team play.

PERFORMANCE STAGE

Under 17

Improving tactical awareness, match performance, physical preparation and competitive readiness.

PATHWAY STAGE

Under 19

Preparing players for advanced opportunities, leadership and the next stage of their sporting journey.

Additional sporting codes may be introduced as the academy grows and resources become available.
